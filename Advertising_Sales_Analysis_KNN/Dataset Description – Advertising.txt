Dataset Description – Advertising & Sales Analysis

Business Context

A company runs advertising campaigns across multiple channels and markets to 
increase product sales. The goal is to understand how advertising investments 
and campaign-related factors influence sales performance and to predict 
expected sales for future advertising budgets.

Each record in the dataset represents a single advertising campaign executed 
in a specific market during a defined time period.

Column Descriptions
1. TV
Description:
Amount of money spent on television advertising for the product during the 
campaign period.

Data Type: Numeric (Continuous)
Business Purpose:
Represents mass media advertising with high reach and strong brand visibility.

2. Radio

Description:
Amount of money spent on radio advertising during the campaign period.

Data Type: Numeric (Continuous)
Business Purpose:
Captures local and regional advertising impact with relatively lower cost.

3. Newspaper

Description:
Amount of money spent on newspaper advertising during the campaign period.

Data Type: Numeric (Continuous)
Business Purpose:
Represents traditional print advertising, often effective for local and older audiences.

4. Region
Description:
Geographical region where the advertising campaign was executed.

Possible Values: North, South, East, West
Data Type: Categorical
Business Purpose:
Accounts for regional differences in customer behavior and purchasing power.

5. Market_Type
Description:
Classification of the market based on population density and urbanization.

Possible Values: Urban, Semi-Urban, Rural
Data Type: Categorical
Business Purpose:
Helps analyze advertising effectiveness across different types of markets.

6. Season

Description:
Time period during which the advertising campaign was conducted.

Possible Values: Festival, Normal
Data Type: Categorical
Business Purpose:
Captures seasonal demand variations and festive buying behavior.

7. Customer_Segment

Description:
Target customer group for the advertising campaign.

Possible Values: Mass, Premium
Data Type: Categorical
Business Purpose:
Differentiates between price-sensitive customers and high-value customers.

8. Campaign_Type

Description:
Primary objective of the advertising campaign.

Possible Values: Discount, Branding
Data Type: Categorical
Business Purpose:
Indicates whether the campaign focuses on immediate sales or long-term brand building.

9. Discount_Percent

Description:
Percentage discount offered to customers during the campaign.

Data Type: Numeric (Discrete)
Business Purpose:
Acts as a direct driver of short-term sales growth.

10. Ad_Frequency

Description:
Number of times the advertisement was shown to the target audience during the 
campaign period.

Data Type: Numeric (Discrete)
Business Purpose:
Measures advertisement exposure intensity and helps identify overexposure risks.

11. Ad_Keyword

Description:
Primary message or theme emphasized in the advertisement.

Possible Values: Discount, Premium, Savings, Luxury, Combo, Launch
Data Type: Categorical (Single-Word Text)
Business Purpose:
Represents the communication strategy used to influence customer decisions.

12. Feedback_Tag

Description:
Overall customer response to the advertising campaign.

Possible Values: Positive, Satisfied, Neutral, Negative, Happy, Expensive
Data Type: Categorical (Single-Word Text)
Business Purpose:
Acts as a proxy for customer sentiment and perceived value.

13. Sales (Target Variable)

Description:
Total sales generated as a result of the advertising campaign.

Data Type: Numeric (Continuous)
Business Purpose:
Primary outcome variable used to measure campaign effectiveness and build predictive models.

Problem Statement

Analyze the relationship between advertising spend, campaign characteristics, 
and market conditions to understand their impact on sales and to predict future 
sales for a given advertising budget.
